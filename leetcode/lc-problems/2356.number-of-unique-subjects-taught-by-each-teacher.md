| 1          | 3          | 3       |
| 2          | 1          | 1       |
| 2          | 2          | 1       |
| 2          | 3          | 1       |

+------------+------------+---------+

+------------+-----+
| teacher_id | cnt |
+------------+-----+

| 2          | 4   |
+------------+-----+
